# Ampwave 1.2 (Build 9)

## What’s New

- CarPlay now shows playlists as soon as they load, includes a Play All action, and continues through the selected album, playlist, song list, or search results instead of stopping after one track.
- Added a compact Lock Screen widget for opening Ampwave quickly.
- Improved purchase restoration and paid-owner recognition for Ampwave’s move to free download. Customers who previously purchased Ampwave keep lifetime access automatically, while new free downloads receive the correct yearly and lifetime options.

## App Review Notes

Ampwave is moving from a paid download to a free download with two ways to unlock the full app:

- A $4.99 yearly auto-renewable subscription with a seven-day free trial for eligible new subscribers.
- A $14.99 non-consumable lifetime purchase.

Customers whose original App Store acquisition predates build 9 are automatically grandfathered into lifetime access using StoreKit 2 `AppTransaction.originalAppVersion`. They do not see a purchase screen and do not need to restore an in-app purchase.

New users can review the purchase options before buying. The yearly button states whether the seven-day trial is available for the current App Store account, and the app does not start a paid subscription unless StoreKit confirms the introductory offer. Restore Purchases restores either an active yearly subscription or the lifetime non-consumable purchase.

This version is configured for manual release so its release can be coordinated with the app’s scheduled change to Free on October 5, 2026.

### Reviewer Notes — Paste into App Store Connect

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing Ampwave 1.2 (build 9).

Ampwave is moving from a paid download to a free download with two StoreKit 2 purchase options:

• Annual subscription: com.ome.Ampwave.annual.subscription. Eligible new subscribers receive a 7-day free introductory trial and then renew annually at the localized App Store price unless canceled.
• Lifetime non-consumable: com.ome.Ampwave.lifetime.access. This is a separate one-time purchase at the localized App Store price.

Customers who acquired an earlier paid App Store build receive lifetime access automatically. Ampwave verifies this from the signed production AppTransaction and classifies original builds below build 9 as paid acquisitions; it does not inspect or store an Apple ID. TestFlight and Sandbox use synthetic acquisition histories, so paid-owner grandfathering can only be fully verified with a production App Store transaction.

No Ampwave account or credentials are required. On a fresh Sandbox account, launch the app to reach the access screen. The annual option should show the 7-day trial and localized renewal price when the account is eligible; the lifetime option should show its localized one-time price. Either purchase unlocks the same complete feature set. Restore Purchases refreshes subscriptions, lifetime purchases, and the original app transaction. If annual access expires, playback is gated, but imported files, playlists, and user data are never deleted.

Ampwave is a local-first player and does not provide a music catalog. Users may import their own audio or optionally connect their own Jellyfin, Plex, or WebDAV server. Optional Last.fm scrobbling and metadata, artwork, and lyrics providers are not required for review.

Version 1.2 also fixes CarPlay playlist loading and queue continuation, adds Play All in CarPlay collections, and adds a circular Lock Screen launcher widget.

Please let us know if any further information is needed.

## Release Cutover Checklist

1. Keep version 1.2 on manual release after approval.
2. Temporarily remove Ampwave from sale in every storefront before releasing build 9.
3. Manually release version 1.2 while the app is unavailable for new acquisition.
4. Wait until the October 5 price change shows Free in every storefront.
5. Restore App Store availability only after both build 9 and the Free price are visible everywhere.

The temporary no-acquisition window is required because Apple applies app price changes at storefront-local times. It guarantees that every paid acquisition has an original build below 9 and every free acquisition starts on build 9 or later.
