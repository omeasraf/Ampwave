# Ampwave 1.3 submission

## What's New (English)

Fixes access for customers who previously purchased Ampwave, restores App Store purchase options, and improves scrolling for long song titles.

## What's New (Japanese)

以前Ampwaveを購入したお客様のアクセスを修正し、App Storeの購入オプションを復元しました。長い曲名のスクロール表示も改善しました。

## App Review notes

Ampwave 1.3 (build 1) is a purchase-access hotfix.

- Existing paid customers are granted lifetime access from Apple's verified production AppTransaction when the original purchase date is before October 6, 2026 at 12:00 UTC.
- Annual subscription: `com.ome.Ampwave.annual.subscription`, $4.99/year in the US, with a one-week free introductory offer for eligible customers.
- Lifetime non-consumable: `com.ome.Ampwave.lifetime.access`, $14.99 in the US.
- The annual subscription, subscription group, non-consumable, and this app version are included together in this review submission.
- No account or login is required. Reviewers can import the attached sample audio files, then open Settings > Ampwave Access to test purchase and restore flows.
- The app never deletes imported music or playlists when access expires.
